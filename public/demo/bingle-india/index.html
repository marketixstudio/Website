Who We Are

India's Most Trusted Glass & Façade Brand

BINGLE INDIA, launched in 2025, is a modern innovation-driven brand transforming the way commercial spaces look, feel, and function. We specialise in façade solutions and interior architectural systems that enhance contemporary office environments.

Founded by Mr. Adish Bingle with over 11 years of industry experience, BINGLE INDIA is backed by the strong legacy of Maruti Industry, established in 1996 — a trusted name in toughened glass manufacturing led by Mr. Deepak Bingle.
