- Worth it / does it work: "is google ads worth it", "does email marketing still work", "is google business profile worth it", "are digital marketing agencies worth it".

- Industry how-to: "how to get interior design leads in india", "how to increase hotel direct bookings", "how much should a startup spend on marketing", "school admission marketing ideas".

Where they are used: the FAQs and answer blocks in content/services.ts, content/industries.ts, the home page and /services/google-ads-ppc. Never answer with invented prices or results; explain what the cost depends on.
