# Hero image prompts (Marketix Studio)

One image per page, for the photo frame on the right of each service, industry and location
hero. Generate them, drop them in a folder, and I will crop, compress and wire them in.

## Format (same for every image)

- **Aspect ratio: 4:5 portrait** (e.g. 1600 × 2000 px, or larger). The frame is 5:6 on
  desktop and 4:3 on phones, so I crop from 4:5 for both.
- **Keep the main subject in the centre third.** Phones crop the top and bottom.
- **Keep the bottom-right corner calm** (about 40% of the width × 30% of the height). The
  "We measure…" card sits there on desktop. Plain wall, floor, sky or soft blur is ideal.
- **No text, logos, brand names, watermarks or readable screens.** Screens should be soft,
  abstract or out of focus.
- **File names:** use the names in brackets below (e.g. `real-estate.png`). JPG, PNG or WebP
  are all fine.

## Style block (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Premium editorial commercial photograph, shot on a full-frame camera with a 50mm lens,
> natural and realistic, shallow depth of field, rich dark tones with soft contrast, subtle
> violet-magenta accent light (#C82AEF) from a practical light source or window reflection,
> modern Indian setting, real people with natural expressions, clean and uncluttered
> composition, subject centred, calm empty area in the bottom-right, vertical 4:5 framing,
> high detail, true-to-life skin tones, no text, no logos, no watermark

## Negative prompt (if your tool supports one)

> text, letters, logo, brand name, watermark, signature, readable screen, UI mock-up,
> cartoon, illustration, 3D render, CGI, plastic skin, extra fingers, distorted hands,
> oversaturated, HDR halo, fisheye, stock-photo grin, cluttered background, low resolution
